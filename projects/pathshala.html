PathShala

An investigation tool for district education officers in Himachal Pradesh: pick a closing school, compare receiving schools side by side, investigate each with agents, and only then choose one. The LLM never produces numbers or approvals.

The decision

Evidence, not just distance and headcount

Consolidating a low-enrolment school affects children who then have to walk further, over terrain, to a school with enough seats. PathShala gives the officer a structured, referenced investigation — and a hard rule: where there is no data it says “Data unavailable” and nothing is invented.

System architecture

Browser-only, with a clean seam for Gemini

The diagram above is the whole system. Three design choices shape it:

◧

Database in the browser

SQLite compiled to WebAssembly (sql.js) loads seed.sql at boot and persists to localStorage (pathshala.db.v7) after every write. The key includes a seed fingerprint, so stale saves are discarded.

⛨

One writer

case/findings.js is the only module that writes cases, evidence and approvals. Tools are read-only (SELECT/WITH only; writes, multiple statements and unknown tables are rejected).

⇄

Provider seam

SimulatedProvider reproduces the prototype exactly. GeminiProvider builds the request already; a /api/agent proxy streams NDJSON tool calls the browser runs on sql.js.

Officer workflow

Choose last

Click a step. The final choice only appears after every option has been investigated.

Choose ONE closing school

Map click or the “needs review” list (≤30 students, or poor/unsafe building). Tick two or more receiving schools. Each candidate gets a 0–100 screening aid — never a recommendation.

score = 100 − 2×road_km − seat_shortfall_penalty − 0.1×(walk_min − 30) − 5×hazards

Side-by-side, nothing chosen yet

Distance and walking time, hazards on the route, free seats (classrooms × planning norm), teachers, building, facilities and citizen feedback — one column per candidate.

walk_min = Tobler(slope) × child_pace (rule R13) free seats = classrooms × max_per_room (R7, derived)

Every school gets its own track

Per-candidate evidence, agent findings, field questions and answers, and interventions. Switch with school tabs or Investigate all. Every step is logged to agent_steps with input/output.

Access Analyst → EvidenceBundle Community Analyst → EvidenceBundle Gap Finder → FieldQuestions Policy Researcher → Interventions

Totals of all options side by side

Questions answered, concerns confirmed, interventions, cost per year, one-time cost, first-year total. The officer picks last.

Referenced, critiqued, approved by a person

Only the chosen school’s report is drafted — every sentence carries a reference — then critiqued, approved and submitted by the officer.

draft_report → sentences[{ text, refs[] }] Report Critic → refs real? numbers in evidence? officer approves → submit

Guardrails

The LLM never produces numbers or approvals

Enforced in code and tested with Playwright (tests/agents.spec.js).

DataAgentHuman
Costs come only from cost_calc (₹6,000 per child from the Samagra norm; ₹9 lakh per classroom from the HP PAB).

Validated output

Every agent output passes Ajv; on failure the runner retries once, then falls back to the simulated result and logs it.

Evidence-bound drafts

The Drafter may only use evidence already in the case; the Critic checks references and numbers.

Humans decide

Only the officer approves, selects an intervention, marks evidence verified, or submits.

Data audit

Every value has a source — or is labelled

Values in the demo may be synthetic, but every field must have a real source and gaps are never filled. Tests fail if removed fields come back.
